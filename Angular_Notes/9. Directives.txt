Directives tell angular how to manipulate, modify, or extend the behaviour or appearance of elements

3 different types of Directives

1. Component Directives
=> @Component({
  selector: 'app-hello',
  template: `<h1>Hello!</h1>`
})
export class HelloComponent {}


here app-hello is an directives


2. Structural Directives
=> *ngIf → conditionally render elements
   *ngFor → loop over lists
   *ngSwitch → switch between views

3. Attribute Directives
=> change the appearance or behavior of existing elements or components.
  *ngClass
  *ngStyle
